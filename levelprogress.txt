================================================================================
                    CYBERNEXUS - LEVEL & PROGRESSION SYSTEM GUIDE
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & LEVELING FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In CyberNexus, every student begins at Level 1 with 0 XP. As you learn cybersecurity
concepts, solve lab scenarios, and capture flags, you earn Experience Points (XP).

The leveling system uses a dynamic scaling formula where the XP required to reach 
the next level increases as your level grows:

   XP Required for Next Level = Current Level * 1,000 XP

Level Progression Breakdown:
  • Level 1  ➜  Level 2 :   1,000 XP required
  • Level 2  ➜  Level 3 :   2,000 XP required
  • Level 3  ➜  Level 4 :   3,000 XP required
  • Level 4  ➜  Level 5 :   4,000 XP required
  • Level 5  ➜  Level 6 :   5,000 XP required
  • Level 6  ➜  Level 7 :   6,000 XP required
  • Level 7  ➜  Level 8 :   7,000 XP required
  • Level 8  ➜  Level 9 :   8,000 XP required
  • Level 9  ➜  Level 10:   9,000 XP required
  • Level 10+: Master Level (unlimited high-tier ranking & leaderboard placement)

When you cross a level threshold, you instantly level up, and any remaining excess XP 
carries over into your progress toward the next level.


2. HOW TO EARN XP (PROGRESSION ACTIVITIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can gain XP through four primary activities in the application:

A) COURSES & INTERACTIVE LESSONS (150 XP - 350 XP per lesson)
   - Read theoretical & practical cybersecurity modules.
   - Complete the end-of-lesson interactive quiz with a score of 70% or higher.
   - Completing an entire course path unlocks a verified Certificate of Completion.

B) HANDS-ON CYBER LABS (50 XP - 150 XP per task / 300 XP - 600 XP per full lab)
   - Deploy active target containers in isolated virtual sandbox environments.
   - Perform live tasks: Nmap port scanning, inspecting HTTP response headers, 
     analyzing log files, or crafting exploitation payloads.
   - Submit valid flag strings to complete tasks and master full lab modules.

C) CAPTURE THE FLAG (CTF) CHALLENGES (200 XP - 800 XP per challenge)
   - Solve real-world vulnerability scenarios across categories like SQL Injection, 
     JWT Manipulation, Cryptography (RSA/AES), Reverse Engineering, and Buffer Overflows.
   - Harder challenges yield higher XP rewards (Easy: 200 XP, Medium: 400 XP, 
     Hard: 600 XP, Insane: 800 XP).

D) ACHIEVEMENTS & BADGES (Bonus XP)
   - Unlock badges by hitting milestones (e.g., "First Blood" for your 1st task, 
     "Script Kiddie" for 250 XP, "Port Explorer" for Nmap labs, "CTF Champion").


3. PROGRESSION TIERS & SKILL RANKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• LEVEL 1 - 3: NOVICE / SCRIPT KIDDIE
  Focus: Fundamentals of Linux CLI, basic networking, HTTP protocols, basic security terminology.

• LEVEL 4 - 6: APPRENTICE / JUNIOR PENTESTER
  Focus: Web application security (SQLi, XSS, CSRF), Nmap network mapping, directory bruteforcing, Wireshark packet capture.

• LEVEL 7 - 9: SPECIALIST / SECURITY ANALYST
  Focus: Advanced exploitation, JWT forgery, binary reverse engineering, cryptography, privilege escalation, SOC log analysis.

• LEVEL 10+: CYBER MASTER / HAX0R GOD
  Focus: High-complexity CTFs, full track mastery, leaderboard dominance, instructor-grade cybersecurity proficiency.


4. ACCOUNT ROLES & PERSISTENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Student: Standard learning account; earns XP, unlocks achievements, and tracks personal progress.
• Instructor: Can create/manage learning content, review student telemetry, and issue certificates.
• Admin: Complete system control, user account management, role assignments, and platform monitoring.

All level state, earned XP, completed lessons, solved CTFs, badges, and certificates 
are automatically persisted to your account session and synchronized across sessions.
